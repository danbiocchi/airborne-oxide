# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in Airborne Oxide.

**Code contributions aren't being accepted yet.** The project is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later, and the copyright holder also offers commercial licenses. To keep that possible, every contributor will need to sign a contributor license agreement (CLA) first. That agreement is still being prepared.

Until then, you're welcome to:

- open issues for bugs, questions and ideas;
- start discussions about the design in [`docs/DESIGN.md`](docs/DESIGN.md).
